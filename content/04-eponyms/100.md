  A: Colles fracture (distal radius with dorsal angulation)
  B: Barton fracture (intra-articular rim fracture with radiocarpal subluxation)
  C: Chauffeur fracture (isolated radial styloid)
  D: Smith fracture (distal radius with volar angulation; "reverse Colles")
explanation: >
  A **Smith fracture** is a distal radius fracture with **volar (palmar)
  angulation** of the distal fragment — the reverse of a Colles fracture, which
  angulates **dorsally**. The mechanism is a fall onto the **back** of a flexed
  wrist (FOOB) rather than an outstretched palm (FOOSH). Smith fractures are
  often unstable and require operative fixation.
