options:
  A: Colles fracture (extra-articular distal radius, dorsal angulation)
  B: Smith fracture (extra-articular distal radius, volar angulation)
  C: Chauffeur (Hutchinson) fracture (isolated intra-articular radial styloid fracture)
  D: Barton fracture (dorsal/volar rim fracture with radiocarpal subluxation)
explanation: >
  The **Chauffeur fracture** (also called **Hutchinson fracture**) is an oblique
  **intra-articular fracture of the radial styloid** in isolation. It was named
  for pre-airbag era chauffeurs whose wrists were struck by a hand crank that
  kicked back on engine backfire. Clinically important to distinguish from a
  **Barton fracture** (rim fracture + radiocarpal subluxation) and Colles/Smith
  fractures (extra-articular, no joint involvement). The scaphoid sits against the
  radial styloid, so scaphoid injury should always be excluded.
