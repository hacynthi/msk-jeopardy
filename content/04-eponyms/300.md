  A: Pseudo-Jones (avulsion) fracture
  B: Jones fracture
  C: Stress fracture of the navicular
  D: Lisfranc injury
explanation: >
  A **Jones fracture** is a transverse fracture at the **metadiaphyseal
  junction** of the 5th metatarsal (within 1.5 cm of the tuberosity). It has a
  high non-union rate due to a watershed blood supply, unlike the more proximal
  avulsion (pseudo-Jones).
