options:
  A: Monteggia fracture-dislocation (proximal ulna + radial head dislocation)
  B: Nightstick fracture (isolated mid-ulnar shaft; no joint dislocation)
  C: Galeazzi fracture (distal-third radial shaft + DRUJ disruption; MUGR)
  D: Essex-Lopresti injury (radial head + interosseous membrane + DRUJ dissociation)
explanation: >
  A **Galeazzi fracture** is a fracture of the **distal-third radial shaft**
  combined with **disruption of the distal radioulnar joint (DRUJ)** — evidenced
  by DRUJ widening, ulnar head subluxation, or ulnar styloid avulsion. Mnemonic
  **MUGR**: **M**onteggia = **U**lna fracture (+ proximal radial head dislocation);
  **G**aleazzi = **R**adius fracture (+ DRUJ disruption). Both require surgical
  fixation. Unlike a nightstick fracture, any isolated mid-shaft radius or ulna
  fracture should prompt evaluation of both joints.
