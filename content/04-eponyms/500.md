options:
  A: Smith fracture
  B: Barton fracture
  C: Chauffeur (Hutchinson) fracture
  D: Die-punch fracture
explanation: >
  A **Barton fracture** is an **intra-articular** distal radius fracture with
  dorsal (or volar) rim involvement and associated **radiocarpal subluxation**.
  The Chauffeur fracture is an isolated radial styloid fracture.
