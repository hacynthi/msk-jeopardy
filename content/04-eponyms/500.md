explanation: >
  **Essex-Lopresti injury** is a rare but critical **longitudinal forearm
  dissociation**: (1) comminuted **radial head fracture**, (2) disruption of the
  **interosseous membrane (IOM)** of the forearm, and (3) **DRUJ dislocation**.
  The devastating pitfall: if the radial head is **excised without recognizing
  the IOM injury**, the radius migrates **proximally**, causing permanent wrist
  and forearm instability. Always image the **entire forearm and wrist** when a
  comminuted radial head fracture is found. The IOM tear is not visible on
  radiograph — widening of the DRUJ and interosseous space on AP forearm views
  are the clues.
