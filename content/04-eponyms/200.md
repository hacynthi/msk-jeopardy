explanation: >
  A **Bennett fracture** is an **intra-articular two-part fracture-dislocation**
  at the base of the **1st metacarpal**. A small volar fragment is held in place
  by the anterior oblique ligament while the metacarpal shaft subluxes **proximally
  and radially**, pulled by the abductor pollicis longus (APL). The **Rolando
  fracture** is the **comminuted** (T- or Y-shaped) variant — same location, worse
  prognosis. Both require surgical fixation.
