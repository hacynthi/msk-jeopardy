options:
  A: Capitellum ossification center — normally appears first (~1 year)
  B: Radial head ossification center — appears second (~3 years)
  C: Medial epicondyle ossification center — appears third (~5 years); can be entrapped in joint
  D: Trochlea ossification center — appears fourth (~8 years); mimicked by entrapped medial epicondyle
explanation: >
  The elbow ossification centers appear in order **CRITOE**: **C**apitellum
  (1 yr), **R**adial head (3 yrs), **I**nternal/medial epicondyle (5 yrs),
  **T**rochlea (8 yrs), **O**lecranon (10 yrs), **E**xternal/lateral epicondyle
  (11 yrs). The critical trap: the **medial epicondyle** appears **before** the
  trochlea. After a valgus stress injury, the medial epicondyle can be **avulsed
  and trapped within the joint**. If the CRITOE order is not applied and the
  trochlea has not yet ossified, the entrapped fragment may be **mistaken for the
  trochlea** — leading to missed diagnosis and permanent disability.
