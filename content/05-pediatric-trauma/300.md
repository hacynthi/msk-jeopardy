answer: C
options:
  A: Buckle (torus) fracture (cortical buckling, no cortex actually breaks)
  B: Complete transverse fracture (both cortices fully disrupted)
  C: Greenstick fracture (one cortex breaks while the opposite cortex bows intact)
  D: Plastic bowing deformity (bowing without any cortical break)
explanation: >
  A **greenstick fracture** is an **incomplete fracture** in which one cortex
  **ruptures in tension** while the **opposite cortex bows but remains intact**,
  unique to the pliable, incompletely mineralized bone of children. Named after
  the way a fresh green stick snaps on one side and bends on the other. It differs
  from a buckle fracture (compression buckling, no frank cortical break) and a
  plastic bowing deformity (no cortical disruption at all). Greenstick fractures
  may require intentional completion to achieve proper reduction.
