  A: Salter-Harris I (fracture through the physis only; often radiographically occult)
  B: Salter-Harris II (fracture through physis + metaphysis; most common type)
  C: Salter-Harris III (fracture through physis + epiphysis; crosses the joint)
  D: Salter-Harris IV (fracture through physis + metaphysis + epiphysis)
explanation: >
  **Salter-Harris (SH) II** — fracture through the **physis and metaphysis**
  (sparing the epiphysis) — is the **most common physeal fracture (~75%)**.
  Mnemonic **SALTR**: I = **S**traight (physis only), II = **A**bove (metaphysis),
  III = **L**ow (epiphysis), IV = **T**hrough (all three), V = **R**ammed (crush).
  SH III and IV cross the joint surface and require anatomic reduction (often
  surgical) to minimize growth arrest and joint incongruity. SH V (crush) has the
  worst prognosis despite a normal-looking initial radiograph.
