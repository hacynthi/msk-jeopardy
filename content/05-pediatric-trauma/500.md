explanation: >
  **Classic metaphyseal lesions (CML)** — "corner fractures" (en face) or
  "bucket-handle fractures" (tangential view) — are **highly specific for
  non-accidental trauma (NAT)**. They result from violent shearing of the
  periosteal-metaphyseal junction during forceful gripping and shaking. Combined
  with **posterior rib fractures** (most specific rib site for NAT), fractures
  of **different ages**, and an **absent or inconsistent history**, CML mandate
  mandatory reporting. OI can mimic NAT but presents with wormian bones, abnormal
  bone density, and a positive family history. Rickets causes metaphyseal
  irregularity from unmineralized osteoid — not traumatic shearing. A complete
  skeletal survey is required per ACR guidelines.
