explanation: >
  Ossification centers appear in the order **CRITOE** (Capitellum, Radial head,
  Internal/medial epicondyle, Trochlea, Olecranon, External/lateral
  epicondyle). A displaced **medial epicondyle** can be trapped in the joint —
  don't mistake it for the trochlea, which ossifies later.
