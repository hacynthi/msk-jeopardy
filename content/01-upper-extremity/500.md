  On the **lateral** view, a **lunate dislocation** shows the lunate tipped
  volarly — the **"spilled teacup"** sign — while the capitate sits dorsally and
  aligns with the radius. In a perilunate dislocation, the lunate stays aligned
  with the radius and the capitate is displaced.
