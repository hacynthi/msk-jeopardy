explanation: >
  **Pubic symphysis widening > 2.5 cm** indicates an **APC (open-book) pelvic
  ring injury**. The pelvic ring **always fails in two places** — the anterior
  symphysis diastasis must have a **posterior counterpart** (SI joint diastasis
  or sacral fracture). APC-II/III injuries involve disruption of the posterior
  SI ligaments and carry significant risk of pelvic arterial hemorrhage. Always
  evaluate the posterior ring on CT. Lateral compression (LC) injuries are more
  common overall; vertical shear implies complete posterior disruption.
