explanation: >
  A **flexion teardrop** fracture is a severe, unstable injury with a
  triangular anteroinferior vertebral body fragment, posterior displacement of
  the body into the canal, and a high rate of **anterior cord syndrome**. It is
  far more ominous than the extension teardrop.
