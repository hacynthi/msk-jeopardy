  A: Anterior compression fracture; stable (anterior column only, posterior cortex intact)

  D: Fracture-dislocation; all three columns disrupted
explanation: >
  By the **Denis three-column model**, an **anterior compression fracture**
  involves only the anterior column (anterior vertebral body wedging with height
  loss). As long as the **posterior cortex (middle column) is intact**, the
  fracture is considered **stable**. Osteoporotic compression fractures are the
  most common spinal fracture and are managed conservatively unless > 50% height
  loss or neurologic compromise is present.
