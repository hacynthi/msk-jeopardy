explanation: >
  A **Jefferson fracture** is a burst fracture of the **C1 ring** from axial
  loading (e.g., diving, head strike). On the open-mouth view, **bilateral
  lateral displacement of the C1 lateral masses** beyond the C2 lateral masses
  is the key finding. The **rule of Spence**: combined overhang > 7 mm indicates
  transverse ligament rupture and **instability**. CT is the definitive imaging
  modality. Despite the dramatic appearance, Jefferson fractures are often
  neurologically intact because the ring fractures outward, expanding the canal.
