  B: Odontoid type II fracture; mechanism is hyperflexion at the dens base
  C: Hangman fracture (traumatic C2 spondylolisthesis); bilateral pars fractures from hyperextension
  D: Clay-shoveler fracture; avulsion of a lower cervical spinous process
explanation: >
  The **Hangman fracture** is a bilateral fracture through the **C2
  pedicles/pars interarticularis** producing **traumatic spondylolisthesis of
  C2 on C3**, classically from hyperextension + axial loading (MVA, judicial
  hanging). Despite the dramatic name, it is often **neurologically intact**
  because the bilateral pars fractures decompress the canal. Classified by
  Levine-Edwards based on angulation and displacement. Unstable types require
  halo or surgical fixation.
