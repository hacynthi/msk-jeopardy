explanation: >
  The **Jones fracture** is a transverse fracture at the **metadiaphyseal
  junction** of the 5th metatarsal (Zone 2, within 1.5–2 cm of the tuberosity).
  It lies in a **watershed zone** between the metaphyseal and diaphyseal blood
  supplies, giving it a high non-union and re-fracture rate — often requiring
  surgery in athletes. The **pseudo-Jones** (Zone 1 tuberosity avulsion) from
  peroneus brevis or plantar fascia pull heals reliably with conservative management.
