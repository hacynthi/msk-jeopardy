explanation: >
  **Lisfranc injury** is disruption of the tarsometatarsal (Lisfranc) joint
  complex. Key radiographic signs: **> 2 mm widening** between the 1st and 2nd
  metatarsal bases, the **"fleck sign"** (small avulsion at the Lisfranc
  ligament attachment between the medial cuneiform and 2nd MT base), and
  malalignment of the **medial border of the 2nd metatarsal with the medial
  border of the middle cuneiform** on AP view. These injuries are commonly missed
  — even subtle diastasis indicates significant ligamentous disruption requiring
  operative fixation.
