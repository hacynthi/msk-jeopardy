explanation: >
  A **Maisonneuve fracture** is a **proximal fibula** fracture associated with
  an unstable ankle injury (medial malleolus fracture or deltoid ligament tear
  with syndesmotic disruption). Always image the **entire fibula** with ankle
  injuries.
