explanation: >
  A **Maisonneuve fracture** is a **proximal fibula** fracture combined with a
  medial ankle injury (medial malleolus fracture or deltoid ligament tear) and
  **complete syndesmotic disruption**. The medial clear space widening (> 4 mm)
  with no visible fibular fracture near the ankle is the classic trap — the
  fibular fracture is at the **proximal fibula**, far from the ankle. Always
  image the **entire fibula** when the medial clear space is widened without an
  obvious lateral malleolus fracture. This is a highly unstable ankle injury
  requiring surgical fixation.
