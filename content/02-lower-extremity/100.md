options:
  A: Weber A fracture (below the syndesmosis, syndesmosis intact)
  B: Weber B fracture (at the level of the syndesmosis)
  C: Weber C fracture (above the syndesmosis, syndesmosis always torn)
  D: Bimalleolar equivalent fracture
explanation: >
  The **Weber classification** categorizes fibular fractures by their level
  relative to the **syndesmosis**: A = below (stable), B = at the level
  (variable stability based on medial-side injury), C = above (unstable,
  syndesmosis disrupted). Weber B is the most commonly encountered type.
