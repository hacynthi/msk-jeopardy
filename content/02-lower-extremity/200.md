options:
  A: Os trigonum syndrome; no further workup needed
  B: Calcaneal fracture; the lumbar spine must also be imaged
  C: Achilles tendon avulsion at the calcaneal tuberosity
  D: Normal Böhler angle; no fracture present
explanation: >
  A **Böhler angle < 20°** (normal 20–40°) indicates a depressed **calcaneal
  fracture**, most commonly from axial loading. Because the same force travels
  up the axial skeleton, always obtain **lumbar spine** radiographs — up to 10%
  of calcaneal fractures are associated with a thoracolumbar burst fracture,
  classically at L1.
